Dear Quality Assurance Team,

I am writing to file an urgent complaint regarding Ciprofloxacin 200mg/100mL IV Infusion (Glass Bottle presentation, Finished Dosage Form), Batch Number CPX24089, Mfg Date: 02/2026, Expiry: 08/2028. 

During pre-administration inspection in our Intensive Care Unit this morning, our clinical nursing staff noticed floating dark brown particulate matter in at least three unopened infusion bottles. The vials were immediately quarantined. Administration was halted before the product was infused into any patients. 

Given that this is a sterile finished injectable product, visible particulate contamination presents an imminent risk of vascular occlusion, emboli, or systemic infection. We require an immediate response regarding health hazard evaluation, potential batch recall actions, and return disposition instructions for our remaining 450 units from this lot.

Please acknowledge receipt immediately and provide our QA reference number.
